## About Me

I'm currently a college student splitting my time between coursework and a pile of side projects I can't stop starting. Most days that means bouncing between independent research and building software just to see if an idea actually works once it leaves my head.

I like figuring things out by doing — pulling something apart, rebuilding it, breaking it again, and learning whatever the process forces me to learn along the way. I don't stick to one stack or one type of project; if something looks interesting, I'll poke at it until I understand it, then usually end up shipping some version of it.

Still early in all of this, still curious, still building.
